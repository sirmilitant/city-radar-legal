Dear Korea Location-Based Service Terms

Effective date: August 24, 2026

These Terms are provided under the Republic of Korea's Act on the Protection and Use of Location Information.

1. Purpose

These Terms govern the location-based features of the Dear Korea application provided by Locale ("we", "the operator"), and set out the rights and duties of both you and us.

2. What the location features do, and cost

① We use the location reported by your device to:

  1. sort nearby places and events and show their distance from you, and
  2. show your current position on the map.

② These features are free. Any mobile data charges are governed by your carrier's terms.

3. How location is obtained and used

① Location is read from your device (GPS and similar) only while you use the Service, and only if you have granted location permission in your device settings. ② We do not receive your location on our servers, do not store it, and do not provide it to third parties. Distance calculation and sorting happen entirely on your device, and the value is discarded afterwards. ③ That said, when the map loads its tiles, the map providers (OpenFreeMap, Naver) can see the map area you are currently viewing, which may be around your position. This follows from displaying a map — we do not send them your location. ④ If we ever add a feature that sends or stores location on a server, we will amend these Terms and obtain your consent before it takes effect.

4. Records of use or provision of personal location information

Because we do not collect or retain location information on our servers, no records of the use or provision of personal location information are created under the Act. There is therefore no such record for you to inspect. Enquiries may be sent to the contact in section 7.

5. Your rights

① You may withdraw location permission at any time in your device settings. ② Withdrawing it only limits the location features (nearby sorting, showing your position); the rest of the Service is unaffected. ③ As no location data is held on our servers, withdrawing permission is sufficient — there is nothing to request the deletion of.

6. Rights of legal guardians of children aged 8 or under

① We do not offer any service that separately collects the personal location information of persons protected under the Act — namely children aged 8 or under, adults under guardianship, and persons with severe intellectual, autistic, or mental disabilities as defined by the Act on Welfare of Persons with Disabilities. ② The Service uses location only on the user's own device and provides no feature allowing anyone to look up another person's location.

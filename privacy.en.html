Dear Korea Privacy Policy

Effective date: December 8, 2026

What changed from the November 5, 2026 version: we added the bot check (Cloudflare Turnstile, USA) in sections 6 and 9, and GitHub, which serves the check page, in section 6. Reason: to keep scripts from making fake accounts.

Locale ("we", "the operator") operates the Dear Korea mobile application ("the Service"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. We follow the Republic of Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and related laws.

Summary

  • There is no sign-up. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or date of birth.
  • You are identified only by a random anonymous ID created on your device.
  • Your location never leaves your phone. We do not send, store, or share your coordinates.
  • We use no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools. We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • The first time the app makes your anonymous ID, a bot check (Cloudflare Turnstile) runs in the background, so scripts cannot make fake accounts. It runs in the USA (see sections 6 and 9).
  • The only things stored on our server are your saved items, your trip plans, the notes you write, the reports you submit, the feedback you send, and error reports when the app crashes (kept for 60 days).

2. Location

If you grant location permission on your device, the Service uses your current position to: ① sort nearby places and events, and ② show your position on the map.

  • Your coordinates are never transmitted to our server, never stored, and never shared with third parties. All distance calculation and sorting happen on your device.
  • That said, when the map loads its tiles, the map providers (OpenFreeMap, Naver) can see the map area you are currently viewing, which may be around your position. This follows from displaying a map — we do not send them your coordinates.
  • You can withdraw the permission at any time in your device settings. Doing so only limits location-based features; the rest of the app works normally.

See our separate Location-Based Service Terms for details.

3. Why we process this information

We use the information only to:

  1. keep your saved list and restore it for you,
  2. publish and review the notes and reports you submit, read the feedback you send to fix wrong details and improve the app, and save, co-edit and share your trip plans, and
  3. prevent abuse and spam, and operate the service reliably.

If the purpose ever changes, we will tell you beforehand and ask for your consent.

4. How long we keep it, and deletion

  • Saved items, notes, and reports are kept until you delete them or ask us to erase them.
  • Feedback messages are kept until you ask us to erase them (section 7).
  • Error reports are deleted automatically 60 days after they are recorded.
  • When you delete a note it stops being visible immediately (it is marked "deleted"). Full erasure happens when you ask us under section 7; until then it is kept hidden for abuse prevention.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device at once. Data already on the server may remain attached to your anonymous ID, so if you want it fully erased please use section 7 below.
  • Erasure method: electronic files are permanently deleted so that they cannot be recovered.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide your personal information to third parties, except where a competent authority requests it through a lawful procedure.

External services contacted while you use the app

Displaying maps and images causes your device to connect to the services below. Because of how internet connections work, your IP address may be visible to them. We do not send them any other information about you.

  • OpenFreeMap (map tiles), unpkg.com (map library)
  • Naver Maps (depending on the map mode in use)
  • Google (place photos), Naver (image thumbnails)
  • The original websites hosting content images (public bodies such as the Korea Tourism Organization, and official sites of individual venues)
  • GitHub (it serves the small bot check page, from the USA)

Once you tap "directions" or "official site" and leave for another app or website, that service's own privacy policy applies.

7. Your rights

You may at any time request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of your information.

  • In the app: tap the heart again to remove a saved item; use the delete button to remove your own note.
  • For anything else, contact slee.rok@gmail.com. Because you are identified only by an anonymous ID, including the identifier shown in the app will let us handle your request faster.
  • We respond within the period required by applicable law.

8. Children under 14

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 14, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn that we have, we erase it without delay.

9. Where data is stored

Service data is stored on servers operated by Supabase Inc. in the Seoul (ap-northeast-2) region in the Republic of Korea. The only data that goes overseas is the bot check data in section 6 (Cloudflare, USA). (Supabase Inc.'s role as a processor is described in section 6.)

10. How we protect your information

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Row-level security in the database restricts each user to their own data.
  • Read permissions are narrowed so that other users' identifiers are never exposed in the app or in API responses.
  • Administrative keys are never bundled in the app; they are used only server-side.
